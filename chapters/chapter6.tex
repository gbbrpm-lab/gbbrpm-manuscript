\section{Recommendations}\label{recommendations}

Grounded directly in the empirical findings, partial hypothesis
outcomes, and Gate~A/Gate~B evidence boundaries established in
Chapters~IV and~V, the study advances six concrete recommendations for
future research, engineering practice, and verification:

\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.,leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Acquire Aligned Independent Outcome Datasets for Gate~B
Domain Validation.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \emph{Urban Drainage:} Partner with DPWH Regional Office~X and
the Cagayan de Oro City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office
to obtain provenance-complete, pre-event blockage surveys paired with
post-storm inundation observations (including verified non-flooded
control junctions) aligned to the exact as-built or Master Plan
conduit topology, enabling confirmatory Gate~B evaluation separate from
Tier-2 controlled injections.
\item \emph{Electrical Distribution:} Obtain event-aligned switching,
protection-trip, and outage logs corresponding to the November~13, 2024
event windows in the Caltech SoCal 28-Bus feeder (or comparable
instrumented radial feeders) so that preregistered event-response
validation can be executed against the verified Component~2 arborescence
baseline.
\item \emph{Software Dependencies:} Evaluate GBBRPM on software
ecosystems where vulnerability advisories ($B_i$) and downstream
real-world exploitability or patch-urgency outcomes are recorded from
independent telemetry sources, ensuring inputs are never reused as
validation labels.
\end{itemize}
\item \textbf{Develop Dedicated Extensions for Cyclic, Bidirectional,
and Hydraulic-Backwater Networks.} Rather than applying the acyclic
GBBRPM~v1 core outside its assumptions or deleting edges heuristically,
future work should formulate a separate \texttt{v2+} extension capable
of handling directed cycles, surcharged hydraulic backwater, meshed
electrical grids, and cyclic package dependencies---for example through
damped fixed-point solvers, strongly connected component (SCC)
condensation with explicit within-SCC physics, or hybrid coupling with
hydrodynamic (SWMM/PCSWMM) and power-flow solvers where bidirectional
redistribution dominates downstream propagation.
\item \textbf{Advance Tighter Dependence-Aware and Hybrid
Monte Carlo Screening Bounds.} Because naive connected-component
ancestry pruning degraded selection regret ($0.5590$ vs.\ $0.00742$
for $R_j$) and Stage~2 tree resolution narrowed under heavy weights
($53.75\%$ at $w=0.75$), future methodological work should investigate
two refinements while retaining GBBRPM~v1 as the benchmark baseline:
(a)~localized $k$-hop or cut-set junction-tree bounds applied solely to
high-overlap reconvergent diamonds (such as \texttt{J\_AGORA\_CONF} or
the \texttt{qs}/\texttt{send} subgraphs), and (b)~adaptive
\textbf{Stage~3 Targeted Monte Carlo Escalation} that allocates
bit-packed simulation trials exclusively to the residual uncertified
boundary band $U_k^{(2)}$ whenever $W_{\max} \ge 0.10$ or
$|U_k^{(2)}| > 0$, combining deterministic screening speed with
user-specified statistical precision.
\item \textbf{Refine Nodewise Distortion Diagnostics Beyond Scalar
$W_j$.} Because $W_j = R_j - M_j$ proved strong as a scenario-level
reversal alarm ($\rho = 0.6769$, $100\%$ recall at $W_{\max} \ge 0.10$)
but weak for within-graph nodewise slack ranking ($\rho = 0.2286$),
practitioners should use $W_{\max}$ strictly as a regime-level trigger
for Stage~2 screening rather than as a nodewise error ranker. Future
research should test whether the Stage~2 tree width $R_j - T_j$ or
inexpensive two-predecessor overlap indicators separate shared-ancestry
slack from independent multi-branch convergence at near-linear cost.
\item \textbf{Harden the Research Prototype for Human-in-the-Loop
Usability and Operational Readiness.} While the current prototype
passed all $76/76$ automated functional, fail-closed validation, and
replay parity checks, it should undergo structured usability evaluation
with domain engineers (drainage, cybersecurity, and power-systems
analysts) and software hardening---specifically interactive
visualization of Stage~1/Stage~2 certified bands ($I_k, O_k, U_k$),
explicit Gate~A/Gate~B warning banners, accessibility compliance,
large-graph layout performance ($|V| \ge 500$), authentication,
persistent scenario storage, and deployment security controls---before
any operational adoption.
\item \textbf{Complete Independent Clean-Environment Replication
(\texttt{DJA}) and Long-Term Archival.} Prior to journal submission and
final archival, an independent team verifier (\texttt{DJA}) should
execute the frozen one-command pipeline
(\texttt{scripts/generate\_\allowbreak paper\_\allowbreak outputs.py})
from \texttt{v2.0.0-heldout-eval} inside a fresh virtual environment
pinned to \texttt{requirements-lock-v2.0.0.txt} and verify bitwise
SHA-256 parity against \texttt{paper\_\allowbreak outputs\_\allowbreak manifest.json}
(with public synthetic and \texttt{Express 4.18.2} artifacts deposited
in an open archival repository while maintaining restricted custody of
raw DPWH and Caltech agency payloads).
\end{enumerate}
