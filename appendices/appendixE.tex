\subsection{Status and Research Role}

This appendix specifies supporting implementation details for the proposed
evaluation. It is a design and verification plan, not a record of tests
completed in this manuscript pass. The prototype will make the model
inspectable; the principal research evaluation will remain the controlled
mechanism comparisons in Chapter III. The member-reported preliminary
implementation evidence remains in Appendix~\ref{app:preliminary}.

\subsection{Returned Trace and Inspectable Quantities}

Synthetic and imported inputs will use the same validated schema. Required
node disturbances and edge susceptibility will be distinguished from missing
observations. Direct susceptibility will be displayed as supplied; loading
and capacity will not be invented for an explicit-$S$ import. An operational
adapter will remain inactive until a suitable dataset is available.

The service will return its topological order and an ordered trace assembled
from engine-computed quantities. Source and aggregate records will identify
the node, local $B$, and resulting $R$; transfer records will identify the
directed edge and its $Q$. Every node will have one source-or-aggregate
settlement record, and every edge one transfer record. A receiving node may
therefore be mentioned in several transfer records. Trace assembly will not
perform a second propagation evaluation; its processing and storage costs
will nevertheless be recorded.

Node and relation inspection will expose susceptibility, incoming
contributions, and the numerical terms of Equations~(3-2) and~(3-4).
Selecting a ranking row will highlight its graph node. Reciprocal selection
behavior will be claimed only if implemented and checked.

\subsection{Replay and Output Agreement}

Replay will be optional and disabled by default. It will use the stored
evaluation, the service's topological order, and predecessor-settlement
dependencies. Independent branches may progress concurrently; a receiver's
final displayed state will settle after all required incoming contributions
arrive. This scheduling will be presentation timing, not a physical process
or an additional model update.

Readouts may interpolate while a contribution is displayed in transit.
Agreement with returned $Q$ and $R$ will be assessed after the corresponding
transfer or aggregation settles. The final states and ranking will agree
across supported display modes and with replay disabled.

The ranking view will be withheld until replay completes or is skipped.
Parameter edits, dataset changes, and disabling replay will cancel the
current replay according to the implemented interface contract. Comparison
and export views will retain their own reported-output behavior. The
supported controls, including reduced-motion behavior, will be documented
for the tested revision rather than assumed from a design description.

\subsection{Planned Checks and Outstanding Evidence}

The verification record will identify the engine version, prototype revision,
fixture inputs, expected quantities, tolerances, and test evidence. It will
cover:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.,leftmargin=*]
\item Numerical and trace consistency: N1--N5 inventory, frozen engine
states, trace determinism and coverage, and reconstruction of selected
receiving states from the returned contributions.
\item Input handling: valid explicit-susceptibility imports; rejection of
unknown endpoints, duplicate records, invalid bounds, cycles, and non-finite
numbers. The team reports that positive infinity in $L$ or $C$ currently
reaches HTTP 500 rather than controlled validation. Regression cases will
cover NaN and both infinity signs in both fields after the defect is fixed.
\item Replay consistency: predecessor settlement, concurrent independent
branches, deterministic scheduling, input non-mutation, and equality of
settled readouts with returned values on controlled replay fixtures.
N1--N5 trace coverage will remain separate from displayed-value checks.
\item Interface integration: frontend build, edit/run workflow, supported
completion and cancellation controls, selection highlighting, containment,
and responsive layout. Automated browser checks will be complemented by
manual inspection of motion and interaction.
\end{enumerate}

The supplied changelog reports revision \texttt{eafec4d}, but the matching
application source and test logs were not included in the manuscript ZIP.
Those reports have not been independently reproduced in this review. Manual
browser inspection is reported as pending. The evaluated revision will need
retained source and test evidence before its checks are reported as completed.
Functional verification alone will not establish usability, accessibility
compliance, security, multi-user operation, or deployment readiness.
