\subsection{Scope and Trace Contract}

This is a supporting design and verification plan, not a completed test
report. Prototype work will be limited to input validation, engine integration,
and inspection of evaluated quantities. The planned mechanism experiments
will take priority over additional presentation features.

Synthetic and imported DAGs will share one schema. Explicit susceptibility
will be displayed as supplied. The service will return its topological order
and engine-computed $B$, $Q$, and $R$ quantities, with one source-or-aggregate
settlement record per node and one transfer record per edge. A receiving node
may appear in several transfer records. Trace assembly will not repeat the
propagation evaluation, but its processing and storage costs will be recorded.

\subsection{Presentation Boundary}

Optional replay will present stored results without changing inputs, final
states, or rankings. Independent branches may progress concurrently; a
receiving state will settle after its incoming contributions arrive.
Interpolated readouts will be presentation values, checked against engine
quantities only after settlement. Display timing will not represent physical
propagation speed. Supported completion, cancellation, and reduced-motion
behavior will be documented for the tested revision.

\subsection{Verification and Outstanding Evidence}

The verification record will retain engine/prototype revisions, fixtures,
expected quantities, tolerances, and test evidence. It will cover N1--N5
numerical agreement and trace coverage, selected receiving-state
reconstruction, valid imports, invalid-input rejection, predecessor-settlement
order, concurrent branches, input non-mutation, and settled-value agreement
across display modes. Frontend build and edit/run checks will be complemented
by automated and manual browser inspection.

Manual browser inspection remains reported as pending. The supplied
revision \texttt{eafec4d} and test claims have not been independently reproduced
from matching application source/logs in this manuscript package. Functional
checks will not establish operational utility or deployment readiness.
