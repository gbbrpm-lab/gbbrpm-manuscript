Probabilistic-sum t-conorm \citep{klement2004} &
$\mathcal{S}_P(x,y)=x+y-xy$;\newline
$1-\prod_k(1-x_k)$ &
Algebraic operator on $[0,1]$; no intrinsic probability or risk meaning &
Bounded in $[0,1]$; commutative, associative, monotone; $0$ neutral &
Local $n$-ary aggregation operator only; no graph topology or pathway dynamics &
$O(d)$ per $d$-input aggregation\\
\addlinespace
Leaky Noisy-OR \& evidential \citep{fallet2012} &
$\Pr(Y\!=\!1\mid\mathbf{x})=$\newline
$1-(1-\ell)$\newline
$\times\prod_{i:x_i=1}(1-p_i)$ &
Conditional event probability or evidential belief mass &
Valid probability in $[0,1]$ under causal independence; correlated parents require joint inference &
Parent-to-child conditional table on a Bayesian\slash evidential DAG; no single-pass scalar index bracket &
$O(d)$ per CPT; joint exact inference exp.\ in treewidth\\
\addlinespace
IC and LT diffusion \citep{kempe2003} &
IC: active $i$ triggers $(i,j)$ w.p.\ $p_{ij}$;\newline
LT: active if $\sum_{i\in P(j)} b_{ij}\ge\theta_j$ &
Stochastic binary activation ($0/1$) and expected seed spread $\sigma(S)$ &
Submodular spread gives $(1-1/e)$ greedy seed bound; no closed-form per-node deterministic bracket &
Multi-hop stochastic pathway spread from fixed seeds; live-edge reachability serves as exact IC reference &
\#P-hard exact; $N$ MC trials in $O(|V|+|E|)$ each\\
\addlinespace
RIPA \citep{faraji2026} &
$R_{\mathrm{pro},ij}=R_{\mathrm{tot}}(i)$\newline
$\times(1-RE_i)(1-RE_j)$\newline
$\times LI_{ij}C_j$;\newline
$R_{\mathrm{tot}}(j)=R_0(j)$\newline
$+\sum_i R_{\mathrm{pro},ij}$ &
Continuous dynamic pathway risk across interdependent infrastructures &
Unbounded above under additive convergence; no receiving-state stochastic bracket &
Recursive multi-hop pathway propagation with multiplicative modifiers and additive node aggregation &
$O(|V|+|E|)$ on DAG; iterative on cyclic graphs\\
\addlinespace
Non-backtracking IC bounds \citep{abbe2017,abbe2020} &
Upper: $m_{i\to j}^{(t)}=$\newline
$1-\prod(1-p\,m^{(t-1)})$;\newline
Lower (DAG): incoming-message bound (Eqs.~21--23) &
Marginal IC activation probability summed to seed-set influence &
Harris--FKG upper bound and DAG lower bound on IC marginals; targets seed spread, not top-$k$ certification &
Multi-hop non-backtracking or DAG message passing; upper messages track path length $t$ &
Upper: $O(T|E|)$;\newline
DAG lower: $O(|V|+|E|)$\\
\addlinespace
MIA and LDAG \citep{chen2010mia,chen2010ldag} &
MIA (tree): $1-\prod(1-p_{ij}\mathrm{ap}_i)$;\newline
LDAG (LT): $\sum_i b_{ij}\mathrm{ap}_i$ &
Approximate activation probability for seed-set influence maximization &
Thresholded pruning breaks strict lower bounds unless unthresholded; LDAG is LT-specific (additive) &
Target-specific pathway restriction: MIA tree removes shared-origin reconvergence; LDAG restricts local DAG &
Local Dijkstra + tree/DAG pass per node\\
\addlinespace
Dynamic causal risk \citep{wu2026} &
Time-varying causal edge weights $w_{ij}(t)$ with weighted causal risk update &
Domain-calibrated operational hazard risk (airport movement areas) &
Empirical causal calibration; no closed-form deterministic bracket or certified top-$k$ pruning rule &
Directed multi-hop causal pathway propagation coupled to operational hazard weights &
Graph/matrix pass per time step\\
\addlinespace
Cascading failure \& load transfer \citep{buldyrev2010,he2019} &
$L_j'=L_j+\Delta L_{ij}$;\newline
node $j$ fails if $L_j'>C_j$ or loses support &
Conserved physical load vs.\ capacity and binary failed/active state &
Percolation/criticality thresholds on ensembles; no continuous comparative-risk bracket on general DAGs &
Direct-neighbor load redistribution upon binary overload/failure; discrete cascade rounds, not continuous risk accumulation &
$t$ rounds of $O(|V|+|E|)$\\
\addlinespace
Domain-coupled drainage models \citep{simone2023,dastgir2023,zhang2025} &
Relevance centrality; runoff edge betweenness + capacity; siltation risk chains &
Drainage-specific criticality, vulnerability, or siltation-chain priority &
Empirically compared vs.\ hydrodynamics; degrades with loops/stress; no cross-domain bracket or top-$k$ certificate &
Global shortest/runoff paths or domain-specific siltation chains; raw physical variables coupled inside ranking formula &
$O(|V||E|)$ to $O(|V|^3)$ (all-pairs paths)\\
