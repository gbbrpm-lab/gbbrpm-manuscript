GBBRPM Cross-Domain Overleaf Project

1. In Overleaf, choose New Project > Upload Project.
2. Upload the complete revised project ZIP.
3. main.tex is the root file.
4. Chapters 1 and 2 distinguish generic formulation, tested cross-domain instantiability, and universal empirical validity.
5. Chapter 3 contains the controlled architecture-validation protocol and the drainage, software-dependency, and electrical-network evaluation methods.
6. Chapter 4 contains the controlled results, robustness and scalability analyses, reconvergence diagnostic, SWMM comparison, software case, and electrical baseline case. Figures 4.2--4.5 can be regenerated from data/core_scenarios.csv with scripts/generate_chapter4_figures.py. The cross-domain figures and tables were generated by scripts/generate_paper_outputs.py in the reproducibility repository.
7. Chapter 5 separates the conclusions supported by each domain and keeps electrical event validation explicitly pending.
8. Untouched generator outputs and their matching reproducibility manifest are under results/paper. The figures used by the manuscript are copied to figures/paper, and presentation-adjusted table copies are under tables/paper.
9. In-text sources use LaTeX citation commands, and references are ordered by first appearance.
10. Formatting in main.tex remains intentionally simple until the official USTP/CITC thesis template or formatting guide is applied.
11. The superseded drainage-only Phase 2 methodology and conclusions remain in the source inside LaTeX false-condition blocks for provenance, but they are not compiled.

Why this helps: equations are native LaTeX, chapter/section numbering is automatic, and later cross-references/citations can be automated cleanly.
